# BTC ×1000

Go long or short on the live Bitcoin price with up to 1000× leverage. Price moves your way, your position multiplies; move against you far enough and it liquidates.
It’s a trading desk distilled to one decision — direction — with the leverage dial doing the rest.

## How a round works

1. Set your stake and leverage (up to 1000×).
2. Open long if you think BTC rises, short if you think it falls.
3. Close manually to lock in profit or loss — or get liquidated if the price crosses your liquidation line.
4. Two fees. An entry fee is charged once when you open, rising with the boost — nothing at ×1, most at ×1000 — and the exact figure for your bet is shown in the ticket before you commit; it comes out of your result at close, so a position opens with it already spent, and it applies to a losing close as well as a winning one. A liquidation costs your stake and nothing more — the entry fee is not taken on top of it. A winning close then pays a share of its net profit, 2% at ×1 rising to 10% at ×1000. Hold past an hour and funding of 0.1% per hour applies to your position size (boost × stake), which is also what eventually liquidates a position left parked.
5. If our price feed stalls, the desk pauses: nothing opens, nothing closes and nothing liquidates while the price cannot be verified. If it stays down for 10 minutes, every open position on that market is closed and your stake is returned in full — no profit, no loss, and no fees. Your history records the outage window.

## Verify a round

Every BTC ×1000 round is derived from a committed server seed, your client seed and a nonce. After the server seed is revealed you can recompute the round with the open verifier: [provably-fair-verifier](https://github.com/betkyo-open-labs/provably-fair-verifier) (also on npm as `betkyo-provably-fair-verifier`). The scheme is explained at [https://betkyo.com/en/fairness/](https://betkyo.com/en/fairness/).

## Odds and derivations

- [When the price feed goes dark: the fairest thing a desk can do is nothing](https://betkyo.com/en/blog/when-the-price-feed-goes-dark/) — Every price-settled game will one day lose its price. Most venues never say what happens then. Ours pauses in both directions, and if the dark lasts ten minutes it gives every stake back — a decision made on the clock, deliberately never on a price.
- [What ×1000 leverage actually means: the one number that decides everything](https://betkyo.com/en/blog/what-1000x-leverage-actually-means/) — Leverage is usually sold as “bigger wins”. It is really a single division: your liquidation sits 1/leverage away from your entry. At ×1000 that is a tenth of one percent — closer than most coins move while you read this sentence.
- [Why our fee rises with leverage — and why we show you the curve](https://betkyo.com/en/blog/why-a-fee-should-rise-with-leverage/) — A flat fee overcharges the cautious and undercharges the reckless.
