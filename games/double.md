# Double

Call the colour of the next roll. Red or black doubles you up; the rare green slot pays ×14.
Double is the simplest streak game on the floor — one colour call, instant settle, repeat.

## How a round works

1. Set your bet amount.
2. Pick a colour — red, black, or green.
3. The roll lands on one colour; green pays ×14.

## How a round is derived

Every Double result is fixed by a hash chain and a public salt committed before bets open; each settled round's chain link hashes to the round before it. The scheme is explained at [https://betkyo.com/en/fairness/](https://betkyo.com/en/fairness/).

**Is Double provably fair?**

Yes. Every Betkyo original commits to its randomness before you bet: a server seed fixed by its SHA-256 hash and mixed with your own client seed, or, for the shared-round games, a hash chain published in advance. The fairness page explains the exact scheme.
